# MATHBOUND: Chronicles of the Infinite Knowledge

An edutainment RPG integrating mathematics with rich fantasy worldbuilding and narrative, built with **Godot 4.7**.

---

## 🌟 Overview

Two interconnected products form the foundation of **MATHBOUND**:
1. **Digital Books**: A 3-layer grimoire (*Chronicle* lore, *Knowledge* mathematical theory, and *Archive* historical field notes).
2. **Digital Game**: A 2.5D isometric adventure inside the fractured Magical Academy Outpost.

### Narrative & Magic System Inspirations
- **Story**: *Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint (ORV)*, *Sousou no Frieren*, *Magi*, and *No Game No Life*.
- **Magic System**: *Witch Hat Atelier* and *Sousou no Frieren*.
  - **The First Rule of Magic**: Humans cannot cast spells alone. Magic Sprites (such as **Auri**) interpret mathematical **Commands** structured at the **Proving Table** and manifest them into physical reality.
  - **The Two-Table System**: 
    1. *The Envisioning Table* (Mental mathematical model & geometric hypothesis).
    2. *The Command Table* (Executing formal Sigils: `Count`, `Add`, `Subtract`, `Multiply`, `Divide`, `GreaterThan`, `LessThan`).

---

## 🎮 Playable Characters

- **Samantha Yu (Age 10)**: Meditative Thinking — *"What does it mean?"* Focuses on underlying assumptions, pattern resonance, and meaning reflection.
- **Miguel Perez (Age 11)**: Calculative Thinking — *"How does it work?"* Investigates mechanisms, procedures, ratios, and structural logic.

---

## 🚀 How to Run the Game

1. Install **Godot Engine 4.7** (Forward+ / Desktop).
2. Open Godot and click **Import**.
3. Select `game-project/project.godot` and click **Import & Edit**.
4. Press **F5** (or click the Play button in the top-right) to start.
   - Press **F11** anytime to toggle between Fullscreen and Resizable Window.
